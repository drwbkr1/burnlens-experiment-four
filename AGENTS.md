# BurnLens Experiment Four repository instructions

These instructions apply to the entire repository. A more specific
`AGENTS.md` may narrow this contract but may not broaden authority, weaken a
hard stop, alter the scientific direction, or override the active milestone.

## Project identity

BurnLens Experiment Four is a prospective fresh-event benchmark intended to
address the weak-data limitation retained by earlier BurnLens experiments. It
is a research and portfolio project, not operational wildfire guidance,
ground truth, an emergency-response product, or a production mapping service.

The canonical repository is `https://github.com/drwbkr1/burnlens-experiment-four`.
The canonical local checkout is `C:\Projects\Active\burnlens-experiment-four`.
All scientific custody, caches, runtimes, temporary files, and outputs must
remain beneath `C:\Projects\Active` and outside Git. OneDrive is prohibited
for project and scientific custody.

## Scientific direction

- Six pilot events are development-only and can never enter the final cohort.
- The final cohort target is 30 new Oregon, Washington, and Idaho fire events,
  split by whole event into 16 train, 6 validation, and 8 sealed test events.
- No event used by Experiments One through Three may enter the cohort.
- Candidate selection and label admission may not use prior model outputs.
- HLS v2 L30 and S30 are the proposed imagery backbone, subject to current
  source, rights, access, integrity, and fitness gates.
- Reference evidence has separate high-confidence, source-dependent, and
  unknown classes. Conflict is masked, not forced into a convenient label.
- The final test opens once only after an exact owner-approved opening bundle.
- Post-opening tuning, event replacement, threshold rescue, or silent
  continuation is prohibited.

## Repository boundary

Commit project-authored software, documentation, manifests, public-safe
receipts, tests, and aggregate evidence only. Do not commit imagery, reference
rasters, labels, arrays, model weights, checkpoints, predictions, credentials,
private review material, or restricted third-party artifacts.

The MIT license applies to repository-authored software and documentation
except where stated otherwise. It does not grant rights to satellite imagery,
reference datasets, labels, models, maps, or other third-party material.

## Operating rules

- Resume from live Git, the active contract, and current records, never memory.
- Execute one dependency-ready evidence unit at a time unless the contract
  proves safe parallelism.
- Record exact inputs, outputs, identities, checks, disposition, exit-condition
  delta, and next dependency.
- Retain failed, invalid, stale, excluded, deferred, and superseded attempts.
- Repair bounded agent-owned structural defects without inventing a human gate.
- Continue automatically through authorized reversible units in a milestone.
- Use issue-backed branches and reviewed pull requests after the explicitly
  authorized unborn-main bootstrap exception.
- Verify external state directly before claiming publication or release.

## Hard stops

Stop for ambiguous rights or terms, credentials or identity custody, spending
or paid services, new source/dependency adoption, normative human reference
decisions, cohort or protocol changes, destructive or irreversible actions,
security testing, authority broadening, sealed test opening, unsupported public
claims, and final scientific release. An explicit active contract may require
additional stops but may not remove these without owner authority.
