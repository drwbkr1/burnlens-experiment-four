# Decision register

## E4-DEC-0001 — Start a clean successor

- **Date:** 2026-08-25
- **Decision:** Build Experiment Four in a new repository rather than reopening
  Experiment Two/2B or extending Experiment Three.
- **Reason:** Preserve prior terminal and failed evidence while addressing the
  weak-data gap prospectively.

## E4-DEC-0002 — Pilot before final cohort

- **Date:** 2026-08-25
- **Decision:** Use six development-only events to establish processing and
  reference rules; permanently exclude them from the final cohort.
- **Reason:** Threshold and workflow feasibility must not tune the final study.

## E4-DEC-0003 — Thirty whole-event final cohort

- **Date:** 2026-08-25
- **Decision:** Target 30 final events with 16 train, 6 validation, and 8 sealed
  test roles.
- **Reason:** Improve event-level evidence while preserving a meaningful sealed
  test and feasible review burden.

## E4-DEC-0004 — One frozen opening

- **Date:** 2026-08-25
- **Decision:** Test evidence opens once after readiness, training, replay, and
  exact owner review.
- **Reason:** Prevent test-driven rescue and preserve a prospective result.

## E4-DEC-0005 — Separate outcomes

- **Date:** 2026-08-25
- **Decision:** Keep dataset, lifecycle, comparison, and release outcomes
  separate and outcome-independent.
- **Reason:** Engineering success cannot substitute for dataset or scientific
  validity, and negative science can still be complete.

- **Date:** 2026-08-29
- **Decision:** End the Experiment Four data route at Milestone 1 as

- **Reason:** The complete frozen universe contains 187 accounted candidates,
