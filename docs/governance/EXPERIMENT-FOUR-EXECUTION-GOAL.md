## Objective

Experiment Four as a prospective fresh-event benchmark that addresses the
weak-data limitation retained by Experiments One through Three.

The primary scientific question is:

> Can fixed burn-change methods generalize across a prospectively selected,
> independently reviewed, whole-event-held-out Pacific Northwest cohort without
> model-assisted event selection, label admission, threshold selection, or
> post-test rescue?

selects the next dependency-ready evidence unit, maintains current and

## Project promise

The project promise is not that a neural model will win. It is that a genuinely
fresh event cohort will be selected without model-output influence, admitted
through source and reference-quality gates, split by whole event, sealed before
evaluation, and used exactly once under a frozen comparison. A dataset or model
failure is a valid result when its evidence is complete and truthful.

A reviewer should be able to determine:

- how the complete candidate universe was formed;
- why each pilot, admitted, excluded, or unavailable event has that status;
- which sources and exact revisions support imagery, geometry, and reference
  meaning;
- which pixels are independently corroborated, source-dependent, conflicting,
  or unknown;
- whether event, scene, geography, time, source, and model-output leakage were
  prevented;
- how training, checkpoint, threshold, and uncertainty rules were frozen;
- how every model seed and test event performed;
- whether the exact result replays; and
- what the evidence does and does not support.

This is not official fire information, field-validated ground truth, an
emergency product, operational wildfire guidance, or a production service.

## Prospective study design

### Cohort structure

- Six development-only pilot events, permanently excluded from the final study.
- Thirty final events from Oregon, Washington, and Idaho.
- Whole-event roles: 16 train, 6 validation, and 8 sealed test.
- No event used by Experiments One through Three.
- No model output, RBR output, prediction, metric, or error surface may inform
  candidate eligibility, event selection, AOIs, labels, or exclusions.

Selection begins from a complete metadata-defined candidate universe and uses
predeclared state, year, fire-size, ecological-setting, maturity, identity,
source availability, non-overlap, and lineage rules. If six pilots plus thirty
final events cannot qualify without relaxing frozen requirements, the route is
`INCONCLUSIVE`; convenient substitution is prohibited.

### Imagery direction

HLS v2 L30 and S30 are the proposed 30 m input backbone, subject to separate
current source gates. Pilot work alone determines the exact temporal windows,
semantic bands, per-pixel QA, compositing, observation counts, registration,
coverage, masks, and unknown behavior. Those rules freeze before final-cohort
acquisition. HLS v1.4 compatibility is not a requirement or rescue route.

### Reference evidence

The target state is `burned`, `unchanged`, or `unknown`.

- High-confidence evidence requires independent corroboration under the frozen
  protocol.
- Source-dependent evidence remains separately identified and cannot silently
  support an independent-accuracy claim.
- Conflicting, ambiguous, temporally unsuitable, poorly registered, cloudy,
  mixed, unavailable, or insufficient evidence becomes `unknown`.
- A human decision must be versioned, hash-bound, and reconciled; a blank or
  informal response is not approval.

Potential external sources, including HLS, MTBS, BAER, aerial imagery, and
ecological strata, remain proposals until separately gated and admitted.

### Model comparison

Only after `dataset_readiness=PASS`, the frozen comparison includes:

1. constant-background;
2. constant-burned;
3. RBR;
4. the Experiment Three 137-parameter architecture retrained from initialization;
5. the canonical Experiment One bounded U-Net retrained prospectively.

Every declared seed and event is reported. Primary reporting includes event-
macro IoU, event-macro Dice, worst-event Dice, balanced accuracy, predicted-

## Leakage and test-opening rules

- Roles are assigned by whole event through one deterministic frozen procedure.
- Training-only data determines normalization and learned parameters.
- Validation-only data selects checkpoints and thresholds.
- Test imagery, labels, and manifests remain isolated from model code and users
  until the one opening.
- The opening requires a complete hash-bound bundle and explicit owner review.
- After opening: no tuning, event replacement, new seed, threshold rescue,
  model replacement, label revision for advantage, or silent successor.
- Correctable implementation defects retain the failed attempt and follow the
  prospectively frozen exception rule; unfavorable outcomes are not defects.
