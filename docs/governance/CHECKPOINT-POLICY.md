# Checkpoint policy

## Operating cycle

1. Verify canonical root, branch, HEAD, worktree, remote, active issue,
   project-control profile, milestone contract, and authority.
2. Select one dependency-ready unit listed as authorized by the validated
   contract.
3. Confirm inputs, allowed writes, checks, recovery path, and stop conditions.
4. Execute the smallest coherent action that answers the unit.
5. Record immutable identities, evidence disposition, exit-condition delta,
   decision value, retained failure, and next dependency.
6. Continue automatically while authority remains active and no hard stop opens.
7. At the milestone boundary, inspect the complete diff and real surfaces,
   reconcile state, and publish the coherent checkpoint when authorized.

## Evidence units and milestones

Evidence units accumulate inside one issue-backed milestone branch. A milestone
changes accepted project truth and receives a reviewed checkpoint. Small diffs,
elapsed time, and record count do not justify publication micro-cycles.

After two consecutive `no_progress` units, run a bounded path-efficiency review.
It may consolidate diagnostics, choose an already-authorized fallback, or close
a dead route. It cannot change scientific scope or a hard stop.

## Status discipline

- Execution status and scientific disposition are separate.
- Missing evidence is `unknown`, not `pass`.
- Preserve failed, invalid, excluded, stale, blocked, deferred, and superseded
  attempts.
- Never date-shop, threshold-shop, replace events, or silently rescue evidence.
- Correct agent-owned structural defects in a versioned successor while
  retaining the defective attempt.

## Publication classes

- The verified unborn-main bootstrap may commit directly to `main` once.
- Later work uses `codex/` branches, pull requests, required checks, merge, and
  live verification.
- Dataset bodies, private review, runtimes, model artifacts, predictions, and
  imagery-bearing renders remain outside Git unless a later contract proves
  rights and explicitly authorizes exact publication.
- A passing local check or CI run is not proof of live publication.

## Human gates

Objective checks, metadata capture, routine QA, evidence recording, and
reversible remediation are not human gates. Human gates are limited to the
classes in the execution goal and current project profile. Review must bind the
complete exact bundle and lock the exact response; blank responses never pass.
