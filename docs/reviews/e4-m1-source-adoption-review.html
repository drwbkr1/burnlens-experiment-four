Rules that become fixed

  • Exact MTBS-to-WFIGS identifier matching; names alone never match.
  • Public, approved, visible, valid, non-quarantined WFIGS records with final-report and fire-out dates.
  • At least 95% EPA Level III coverage; largest-area code is dominant.
  • Both HLS collections need pre- and post-fire granule metadata in fixed broad intervals; no quality or pixel inspection.
  • All 68 prior-experiment identities remain excluded.
  • MTBS scene IDs, burn indices, severity thresholds, and analyst comments are denied from intake.
  • Complex members count as one selectable slot; probable geometry duplicates are not eligible.

Terminal shortfall: fewer than 12 selectable slots in any state, fewer than 36 total, or failure of the fixed ecology minima ends Milestone 1 INCONCLUSIVE. The years, size, states, prior-event exclusion, and sources cannot be relaxed to rescue it.
